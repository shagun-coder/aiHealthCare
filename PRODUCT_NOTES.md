
# Vitals UI / Product Notes

## Visual language

- Deep blue-slate background for calm, high-contrast monitoring.
- Cool ivory and gray typography.
- Amber is reserved for early warnings.
- Sage-gray indicates a stable signal without presenting wellness claims.
- Thin astronomical rings and star-field motifs remain as product identity.
- Dense information is avoided on the primary monitoring card.

## Core user journey

1. Email/password authentication.
2. Daily risk-monitoring dashboard.
3. Personal baseline comparison.
4. AI Insight with current risk interpretation.
5. Trends over 7/30/90 days.
6. Wearable connection, early warnings, notifications, security, and AI personalization settings.
7. Monitoring continuity during heat waves, pollution events, floods, and unreliable connectivity.

## AI UX

The risk card deliberately exposes:
- current risk score,
- model confidence,
- key factors,
- baseline comparison,
- on-device processing indicator.

## Scope boundary

This is a monitoring device, not a wellness device. Sleep, stress, breathing, and blood-pressure tracking are out of scope. Risk status is informational and must not be presented as a medical diagnosis or certainty.
